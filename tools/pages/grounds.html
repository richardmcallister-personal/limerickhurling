Where Limerick have played

Every ground in the archive, sized by how often Limerick played there. Tap a pin for the record. Thurles Sportsfield counts as Semple Stadium, Jones's Road as Croke Park, and the Cork Athletic Grounds as Páirc Uí Chaoimh, which replaced it on the same site.

Hollow pins mark the town only, where the ground itself couldn't be placed. Map © OpenStreetMap contributors.
